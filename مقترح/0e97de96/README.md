## Evidence rules used in this proposal

- Every number, step order, dose, license or product claim has an inline source URL.
- Primary sources are preferred (guidelines, peer-reviewed papers, textbooks, official license / engine pages).
- `(uncertain)` = weak or secondary source; `UNVERIFIED` = a license or fact that could not be confirmed.
- Step 2 re-checks the most important claims and records a verdict for each (confirmed / corrected / unverified).
